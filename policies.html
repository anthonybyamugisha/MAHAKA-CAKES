The fine print, in plain language

Policies

Delivery, returns, your privacy and the terms of ordering. We have kept every section short and written it the way we would explain it in person.

Delivery and collection

Applies to all orders

Where we deliver

  • MUBS Jinja Campus: UGX 5,000 per order.
  • Agreed nearby areas: quoted per order before you confirm.
  • Collection: free, from our agreed pickup point on campus.

How long it takes

  • Standard cakes: at least 24 hours’ notice.
  • Custom theme cakes: at least 48 hours’ notice.
  • Bulk and event orders: we agree the time when we quote.

Opening hours

Monday to Saturday, 8:00 am to 7:00 pm. Sunday orders are by arrangement.

If we are late

If we cannot deliver at the agreed time, we tell you as soon as we know and agree a new time, or refund any payment made for that order.

Returns, cancellations and refunds

Because every cake is made to order

Cancelling an order

  • Cancel more than 24 hours before the agreed time: full refund.
  • Cancel within 24 hours, before baking starts: we refund what you paid, less any ingredients already bought for a custom order.
  • After baking has started: the order cannot be refunded.

If something is wrong

Check the cake when you receive it and tell us on the same day if something is wrong. We will replace it or refund you. Please keep the cake and a photo so we can see the problem.

What we cannot refund

Damage caused after the cake has been handed over, or a change of mind after baking has started, cannot be refunded.

Privacy notice

Written with Uganda’s Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 in mind

What we collect

  • Your name, so we know whose order it is.
  • Your phone number or WhatsApp contact, to confirm and arrange the order.
  • Your delivery area or pickup preference.
  • The details of your order: cake, size, date and message.

Why we collect it

Only to prepare, confirm, deliver and keep a record of your order. We do not collect information we do not need, and we do not ask for national ID numbers or bank passwords.

Who can see it

Only the MAHACA Sweet Cakes members who handle orders, payments and delivery. We do not sell, rent or publish customer information, and we do not forward it to other businesses.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for six months after the order, so we can handle any complaint. After that they are deleted. Messages in a WhatsApp chat follow WhatsApp’s own rules.

Your rights

You may ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us on WhatsApp or by email and we will respond within a reasonable time. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Office of Uganda.

What we will never do

We will never publish a customer’s phone number, message or photo without their permission, and we will never claim a review that a customer did not write.

Terms of ordering

Short version

  • Prices: all prices are in Ugandan shillings and include the size stated. Delivery is extra.
  • An order is confirmed only when we reply in writing with the final price and time.
  • Payment: we may ask for payment before baking. We never accept a screenshot alone as proof.
  • Changes: tell us as early as possible. Changes after baking has started may not be possible.
  • Allergies: our kitchen handles wheat, eggs, milk and nuts. Tell us before ordering if you have an allergy.
  • Photos: we may photograph a cake for our pages. We will not show your face or name without permission.
  • This website is a student project for BUC3106 at MUBS. Payments shown are not active.

Our claims and the proof

We only say what we can show

  • “Made to order” — we bake after the customer confirms, not in advance.
  • “Fresh” — the cake is baked on the day it is collected or delivered.
  • “Prices shown openly” — every cake on this site has a price and an item code.
  • “Campus delivery” — we deliver within MUBS Jinja Campus for the stated fee.

We do not display customer reviews, awards or certificates, because we have not earned them yet. When real customers send us feedback, we will publish it with their permission and keep the original message as proof.

If someone pretends to be us

Fake accounts and fake reviews

We keep a list of our official pages and link to them from this website. If you see an account using the MAHACA Sweet Cakes name, logo or photos that is not linked here, please tell us on our official WhatsApp number.

We will report the account to the platform, ask for it to be removed, and warn our customers in a public post. We will never ask for payment through a new or unusual number without confirming it in our official chat first.
